# Teaching

At UCSB I teach courses on migration, Middle Eastern politics, and causal inference. In the 2025-26 academic year I will teach a upper-division undergraduate course on Middle Eastern politics.

- "Introduction to Causal Inference" (undergraduate, PS 103, fall 2026)
- "Introduction to Comparative Politics" (undergraduate, PS 6, spring 2027)
- “Causal Inference: Design and Methods” (graduate, PS 207, spring 2027)

Past courses at UCSB include:

- “Introduction to Middle Eastern Politics” (undergraduate, PS 150A, most recently spring 2026)
- “Political Science Senior Honors Program” (undergraduate, PS 197A, 197B and 197C, most recently academic year 2024-25)
- “Migration and Forced Displacement” (senior seminar/graduate, PS 196/594, most recently spring 2022)
